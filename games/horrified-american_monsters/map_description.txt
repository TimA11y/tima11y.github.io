Map Description

The center of town is a loop of six connected locations: High School, North Intersection, Gas Station, City Hall, Town Center, and South Intersection, each one connected to the next, forming a closed loop back to High School.

Two smaller loops branch off that central loop. First: City Hall, Gas Station, Diner, and Tavern form a second loop, giving two ways to get between City Hall and Tavern. Second: High School, Sheriff's Station, Motel, and North Intersection form a third loop, giving two ways to get between High School and North Intersection.

Everything else is a dead-end branch off the center, and each of these locations connects to only one other place, so once you leave the ring you can't loop back — you have to retrace your steps to return.

Off North Intersection, by way of Cabin: Cabin connects to Abandoned Mine and Campground, both dead ends.

Off Diner, by way of Ranger Station: Ranger Station connects to Mansion and Trail Head, both dead ends. Diner also connects directly to General Store, a dead end.

Off Tavern: Hospital is a dead end.

Off Town Center: Farm and Cemetery are both dead ends.

Off South Intersection: Bait Shop is a dead end. South Intersection also connects to Resort, which connects one step further to Swimming Hole, a dead end.

Off High School directly: Stadium is a dead end.

In short: one six-location ring at the center, two smaller loops attached to that ring, and eleven single-connection dead ends branching off in short chains of one or two steps.
